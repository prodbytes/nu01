# Languages

- **Bundles:** `site/i18n/<lang>.json`, flat key → text maps: `en`
  (English, the default), `es` (Spanish, Spain), `pt` (Portuguese, Brazil)
  and `ca` (Catalan). Each bundle has `lang`, `locale` and `name` (the
  language's own name, shown in the footer). Values may hold inline HTML
  (`<strong>`, `<a>`, `<span class="accent">`); `meta.*` and the
  accessibility labels are escaped as plain text.
- **Build:** `scripts/build.py` fails unless every bundle has exactly
  `en.json`'s keys, none empty, and every placeholder in
  `site/index.html` resolves. Adding a language is adding a bundle.
- **Pages:** English at `/`, the others at `/<lang>/`, each with
  `<html lang>`, a canonical URL, `hreflang` alternates for every language
  plus `x-default` (`/`), and `og:locale`. `sitemap.xml` lists them all.
- **Picking a language:** the footer links switch language and remember the
  choice (`localStorage` `nu01.lang`). On a first visit to `/`, a browser
  whose preferred languages include Spanish, Portuguese or Catalan (before
  English) is sent to that page once. Without storage nothing is remembered
  and nobody is redirected.
- Product names (presence, uplink, rbacr) and "LEARNWARE" stay untranslated.
